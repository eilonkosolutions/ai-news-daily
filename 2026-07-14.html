Xi Jinping to deliver first-ever keynote at China's flagship AI summit

2026-07-13 policy governance china

China's government confirmed that President Xi Jinping will attend and deliver the keynote address at the opening ceremony of the 2026 World AI Conference and High-Level Meeting on Global AI Governance in Shanghai, running July 17–20 — the first time China's top leader has personally headlined the event. State media say Xi will "systemically elaborate" on China's AI development and governance positions, and analysts expect him to use the platform to give shape to Beijing's proposed World AI Cooperation Organization, a China-headquartered international body it has been pushing as a counterweight to Western-led AI governance efforts.

Why it matters

A head-of-state keynote at a national AI conference is a significant escalation in how directly governments are now positioning themselves in the global AI race, and signals China's intent to compete with the US not just on models and chips but on setting the terms of international AI governance.

Meta's Louisiana AI supercluster investment nearly doubles to more than $50 billion

2026-07-13 infrastructure compute meta

Meta confirmed its Richland Parish, Louisiana "Hyperion" data center — being built with joint-venture partner Blue Owl Capital — is being expanded to a 5-gigawatt, nearly 10-million-square-foot facility with total investment now exceeding $50 billion, up sharply from the $27 billion figure disclosed when the joint venture was formed in October. The project, which started at an estimated $10 billion when it broke ground in December 2024, is targeted to reach 2 GW of capacity by 2030, with no confirmed date yet for the full 5 GW buildout. Meta says the expansion includes more than $1 billion in local infrastructure upgrades.

Why it matters

The repeated upward revisions to a single supercluster's price tag illustrate how fast AI infrastructure cost estimates are escalating industry-wide, adding to investor scrutiny of whether current AI capital spending plans are sustainable.
