Anthropic in talks to acquire Israeli AI startup Decart for $6 billion

2026-08-13 M&A Anthropic compute IPO

Anthropic is reportedly in talks to acquire Decart, an Israeli AI startup, for roughly $6 billion — what would be Anthropic's largest acquisition to date. Decart, founded by brothers Dean and Orian Leitersdorf and Moshe Shalev, builds two things Anthropic wants: a GPU optimization layer (the "Decart Optimization Stack") for cutting inference and training costs across hardware platforms, and real-time generative-video "world models," including its Lucy system for live video manipulation. The deal would value Decart at about 50% above the $4 billion mark it set in a $300 million raise this past May, and comes as Nvidia, SpaceX, and Amazon were also reportedly circling the startup. No agreement is final and talks could still fall apart, but the move fits Anthropic's broader pre-IPO push to control compute costs ahead of an expected autumn listing.

Why it matters

It signals that compute efficiency itself has become a strategic acquisition target for frontier labs, not just a vendor relationship — with Anthropic willing to spend billions to own the optimization layer outright rather than license it.

OpenAI's annualized revenue run rate tops $40 billion ahead of IPO

2026-08-13 business OpenAI IPO revenue

OpenAI's annualized revenue run rate has surpassed $40 billion, roughly double its pace at the end of 2025, Bloomberg reported. President Greg Brockman told staff internally that the company's monthly revenue run rate grew more than 20% in July alone, with business-customer revenue up 32% month-over-month. The growth is credited to subscription sales, a nascent advertising business, and enterprise coding tools including Codex and ChatGPT Work. The figures come as both OpenAI and Anthropic have filed confidential paperwork for a stock-market listing, with Anthropic expected to go public as soon as this fall — ahead of OpenAI.

Why it matters

The run rate confirms OpenAI's growth hasn't slowed even as rivals ship competitive models monthly, and sets a high revenue bar that its eventual IPO prospectus will be measured against.

OpenAI and Cerebras launch "Ultrafast" mode for GPT-5.6 Sol, up to 14x faster

2026-08-13 models product-launch OpenAI inference Cerebras

OpenAI began a limited preview of "Ultrafast," a new API service tier that runs GPT-5.6 Sol up to 14x faster than its Standard tier, generating as much as 750 output tokens per second with the same underlying intelligence. The speed comes from running on Cerebras's wafer-scale chips (WSE-3), which keep the model's full parameters resident in on-chip SRAM to eliminate weight-loading latency. OpenAI says the mode is roughly 5x faster than Claude Opus 4.8's Fast mode and 11x faster than Claude Fable 5; it is currently available only to a select group of API customers, with no separate Ultrafast pricing published yet beyond Sol's standard $5/$30 per-million-token rate.

Why it matters

It's a sign the frontier race is expanding beyond raw capability and price into raw latency, with specialized inference silicon (Cerebras, Groq, and similar) becoming a competitive differentiator labs are willing to partner around rather than build alone.

OpenAI ו-Cerebras משיקות מצב "Ultrafast" ל-GPT-5.6 Sol, מהיר פי 14

2026-08-13 מודלים השקת מוצר OpenAI הסקה Cerebras

OpenAI פתחה תצוגה מקדימה מוגבלת של "Ultrafast", שכבת שירות חדשה ב-API שמריצה את GPT-5.6 Sol במהירות של עד פי 14 מהשכבה הרגילה, ומייצרת עד 750 טוקנים בשנייה תוך שמירה על אותה רמת אינטליגנציה. המהירות מושגת הודות להרצה על שבבי הפלטה המלאה (WSE-3) של Cerebras, שמחזיקים את כלל פרמטרי המודל בזיכרון SRAM על-שבבי כדי לבטל את זמן ההשהיה הכרוך בטעינת משקלים. OpenAI מציינת כי המצב מהיר פי חמישה בערך ממצב ה-Fast של Claude Opus 4.8, ופי 11 מ-Claude Fable 5; הוא זמין כרגע רק לקבוצה נבחרת של לקוחות ה-API, וטרם פורסם תמחור נפרד למצב Ultrafast מעבר לתעריף הרגיל של Sol — 5$/30$ למיליון טוקנים.

למה זה חשוב

זהו סימן לכך שהתחרות בחזית הבינה המלאכותית מתרחבת מעבר ליכולות גולמיות ומחיר אל עבר זמן תגובה גולמי, כאשר שבבי הסקה ייעודיים (Cerebras, Groq ודומיהם) הופכים ליתרון תחרותי שהמעבדות מוכנות לשתף פעולה סביבו במקום לבנות לבד.
