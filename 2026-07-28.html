Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 open weights go live — the largest open-weight release yet

2026-07-27 open-weight models china

Update: Kimi K3's open weights, still unpublished when we checked yesterday morning despite a stated 00:00 UTC July 27 target, went live on Hugging Face (moonshotai/Kimi-K3) hours later. The release makes the full 2.8-trillion-parameter model freely downloadable under a modified MIT license — real safetensors shards, a LICENSE file and a complete model card, around 594GB for the native MXFP4-quantized release (a gated download requiring license acceptance). K3 combines native multimodal vision with a 1-million-token context window; independent arena evaluations have it beating both companies' prior-generation systems on coding and agentic tasks, trailing only Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 and OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol overall.

Why it matters

It's now the largest open-weight model ever released, putting near-frontier capability into anyone's hands for free and sharpening the pressure on closed labs' pricing just as 25 companies publicly lobbied Washington this week not to restrict open weights.

Nvidia in talks to guarantee $250B in financing for OpenAI's 10-gigawatt Ohio data center

2026-07-27 infrastructure funding openai nvidia

Nvidia is in talks to guarantee roughly $250 billion in financing so OpenAI can lease a planned 10-gigawatt data center that SoftBank's SB Energy subsidiary is developing on the site of a former uranium-enrichment plant near Piketon, Ohio, the Wall Street Journal reported. Because OpenAI has no investment-grade credit rating of its own, Nvidia would act as guarantor, letting SB Energy raise the roughly $500 billion in debt the build is expected to cost. In a separate, parallel negotiation, Nvidia is also discussing financing up to $350 billion of the chips needed to fill the campus. Neither company has confirmed final terms, and the talks could still change.

Why it matters

If finalized, it would be one of the largest financing arrangements in tech history and a template for chipmakers underwriting compute buildouts labs can't yet fund on their own balance sheets.

Nvidia takes a $1B, 4.5% stake in South Korea's Naver to fund an "AI factory"

2026-07-27 infrastructure investment korea sovereign-ai

Nvidia agreed to buy about $1 billion of newly issued Naver shares — roughly 7.24 million shares at 204,500 won ($140) each — for a 4.5% stake, making it Naver's third-largest shareholder behind South Korea's National Pension Service and BlackRock. It's Naver's first share sale via private placement in 22 years. The money funds a planned AI factory at Naver's GAK Sejong hyperscale data center, sized for about 100,000 Nvidia GPUs (Blackwell and Vera Rubin), with Canadian firm Brookfield separately discussing up to $9 billion in financing for the site.

Why it matters

It extends Nvidia's pattern of taking equity stakes directly in the cloud and sovereign-AI operators building capacity for its chips, tying its own growth to the infrastructure buildout it sells into.
