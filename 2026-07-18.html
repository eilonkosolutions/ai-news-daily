Moonshot AI releases Kimi K3, a 2.8-trillion-parameter open model rivaling Claude and GPT

2026-07-16 models open-weight china

Moonshot AI released Kimi K3, a 2.8-trillion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model (896 experts, 16 active per token) built on a new hybrid linear-attention architecture the company calls Kimi Delta Attention, with native vision support and up to a 1-million-token context window. Moonshot says K3 performs competitively with Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 and substantially outperforms Claude Opus 4.8 and OpenAI's GPT-5.6 on its own benchmarks, and it currently leads the Frontend Code arena ahead of Fable 5. It's available now via API at $3/$15 per million input/output tokens — the highest price of any Chinese lab, though still roughly half Opus 4.8's per-task cost — with open weights promised by July 27.

Why it matters

It's the largest open-weight model released to date and the clearest sign yet that Chinese labs are closing the gap with US frontier models on capability, not just cost, intensifying pressure on Western labs' pricing and open-weight strategies.

China formally launches WAICO as 29 nations sign founding agreement, UN chief attends

2026-07-16 policy china ai-governance

Update since our July 17 report on Xi Jinping's WAIC keynote: the World AI Cooperation Organization is no longer just a pitch — 29 countries, including Russia, Brazil, Indonesia, Malaysia, South Africa, Senegal, and Pakistan, formally signed WAICO's founding agreement in Shanghai on July 16, the eve of the World AI Conference, with UN Secretary-General António Guterres in attendance. Xi called it "an important milestone in the history of AI development" in his keynote the next day and pledged 5,000 AI training and seminar opportunities for developing nations through the new body, whose stated goals are promoting international AI cooperation and shared regulation.

Why it matters

A signed, 29-member founding agreement — with the UN chief present — turns China's AI-governance ambitions from rhetoric into a standing institution, giving Beijing a concrete forum to compete with US-led AI governance efforts for influence over the Global South.

Fireworks AI raises $1.5B Series D at $17.5B valuation on surging inference demand

2026-07-16 funding infrastructure inference

Fireworks AI, an enterprise inference platform for open and custom models, raised a $1.505 billion Series D at a $17.5 billion valuation, led by Atreides Management, Index Ventures, and TCV with participation from Lightspeed Venture Partners and NVIDIA. The company says it has surpassed $1 billion in annualized revenue run rate, up 5x year-over-year since its last round, while daily token volume served on its platform nearly tripled from about 15 trillion to more than 40 trillion tokens.

Why it matters

The round is one of the largest yet for an AI infrastructure company that doesn't build its own frontier models, underscoring how much investor money is now chasing the inference-serving layer as enterprises deploy open and specialized models at scale.
