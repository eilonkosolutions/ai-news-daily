OpenAI proposes 5% US government equity stake days after GPT-5.6 access was restricted

2026-07-02 policy industry frontier labs regulation

The Financial Times reported that OpenAI has discussed giving the US government a 5% equity stake, worth an estimated $42.6 billion at the company's $852 billion valuation. CEO Sam Altman has reportedly pitched a broader "Public Wealth Fund" concept, modeled on the Alaska Permanent Fund, under which every leading US AI developer would contribute a similar share of equity; he has discussed the idea with President Trump, Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, and Senator Bernie Sanders. The talks emerged six days after the US government asked OpenAI to delay the full public launch of GPT-5.6, limiting initial access to a small number of vetted partners over concerns about the model's advanced capabilities. The FT described the equity discussions as early-stage and conceptual, noting any formal arrangement would likely require an act of Congress.

Why it matters

It would be an unprecedented restructuring of the relationship between a frontier AI lab and its government, blending industrial policy, national security oversight, and equity ownership in a way that could set a template for how advanced model releases are governed going forward.

Anthropic restores Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 globally after 19-day export-control suspension

2026-07-01 models policy security frontier labs

The US Commerce Department lifted export controls on Anthropic's Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models on June 30, roughly two and a half weeks after imposing them over a jailbreak vulnerability that Amazon researchers had discovered, which could get the model to describe or write code exploiting a software flaw. The original order, issued June 12, required Anthropic to suspend foreign-national access; Anthropic instead shut both models down globally, citing technical and legal constraints. Fable 5 returned worldwide across Claude.ai, the Claude Platform API, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork on July 1, while Mythos 5 — which carries fewer safety restrictions — is being restored to a set of US organizations. In exchange, Anthropic agreed to proactively detect and report security risks and help develop release standards for future frontier models; a new classifier reportedly blocks the jailbreak in over 99% of attempts.

Why it matters

It's one of the first cases of a government export-control order forcing a global shutdown of a deployed frontier model over a safety vulnerability, and its resolution — via a negotiated safety-reporting arrangement — is likely to inform how future jailbreak discoveries are handled industry-wide.

Together AI raises $800M Series C at $8.3B valuation as open-source inference demand surges

2026-07-01 funding infrastructure open-weight

Together AI, which rents out Nvidia GPU clusters and serves open-weight models, closed an $800 million Series C led by Aramco Ventures, with participation from Vista Equity Partners, General Catalyst, Nvidia, and others, at an $8.3 billion post-money valuation — more than double its $3.3 billion valuation from early 2025. Alongside the equity raise, investors committed over 500 megawatts of compute capacity, and the company reported Q1 annual contract value exceeding $115 million, crediting demand from enterprises seeking cheaper alternatives to closed frontier models.

Why it matters

The scale of the raise and the compute commitments underscore how much capital is still flowing into inference infrastructure specifically for open-weight models, a distinct track from the spending on proprietary frontier labs.

היקף הגיוס וההתחייבויות לכושר מחשוב מדגישים כמה הון ממשיך לזרום לתשתיות הסקה (inference) המיועדות במיוחד למודלים במשקלים פתוחים — מסלול נפרד מההשקעות במעבדות חזית קנייניות.
