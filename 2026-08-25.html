Alibaba launches Wan3.0 AI video model days after pricing a $10.2 billion share sale for AI infrastructure

2026-08-24 models video-generation funding China

Alibaba released Wan3.0, its newest AI video-generation model, able to produce 30-second clips (double its predecessor Wan2.7) and to generate video directly from documents, spreadsheets, slides, and web pages. The launch lands two days after Alibaba priced a HK$80 billion (~$10.2 billion) placement of 710 million new shares in Hong Kong, the largest primary follow-on equity offering ever done by a Hong Kong-listed company, with 100% of proceeds earmarked for AI infrastructure and compute. The move follows Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Max release earlier this month and comes as its AI Cloud revenue has been growing roughly 45% year-on-year.

Why it matters

It's a direct, real-time link between capital markets and model shipping — Alibaba is raising record equity specifically to fund AI compute and using it to back an aggressive release cadence, underscoring how central generative video and cloud AI capacity have become to the competitive race among Chinese frontier labs.

XPeng's humanoid robotics unit raises over $900 million in its first external funding round

2026-08-24 robotics funding humanoid physical-AI

Chinese EV maker XPeng's robotics division signed equity financing agreements raising more than $900 million, valuing the unit at over $6.3 billion post-money — what XPeng describes as the largest single private-equity round yet in China's embodied-intelligence sector. The round was led by IDG Capital and Gaorong Ventures, with Tencent and Alibaba participating as strategic investors, ahead of planned humanoid-robot production.

Why it matters

It signals investor capital is now flowing into "physical AI" — humanoid robotics and embodied intelligence — at a scale that mirrors the funding frenzy around large language models, with major tech platforms (Tencent, Alibaba) hedging across both software and hardware AI bets.
