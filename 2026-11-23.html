Trump threatens a "tremendous tax" on US companies that build AI data centers overseas

2026-11-20 policy data centers infrastructure catch-up

President Trump said at a White House briefing on Friday, Nov. 20, that US companies building AI data centers abroad will face a "tremendous tax," framing the threat as a way to keep AI computing capacity in the United States and out of China's reach. He gave no rate, no list of covered companies and no timeline, did not say how the tax would treat projects already under construction, and acknowledged he had not yet briefed his own officials on the idea. Alphabet is seen as the most obvious target after Trump criticized its planned multibillion-euro data-center investment in Finland in September. The threat landed on the same day as a broad AI-led stock selloff and goes well beyond the Commerce Department's new chip-licensing rule, which targets overseas data centers tied to China rather than offshore construction itself. Reporting links the move to voter backlash against data centers in the US midterms.

Why it matters

US hyperscalers are planning large AI data-center build-outs in Europe, the Middle East and Asia, and a tax on building abroad, if enacted, would reshape where the world's AI compute gets built and add policy risk to already stretched capital plans.

Amazon nears $7.5 billion deal to acquire AI efficiency startup Decart, FT reports

2026-11-21 acquisitions amazon aws chips

Amazon is in advanced talks to buy Decart for about $7.5 billion, and a deal could be announced as early as this week, the Financial Times reported on Saturday, Nov. 21; Amazon did not comment and Decart declined to comment. The Wall Street Journal first reported talks in October at around $7 billion. Decart, founded in 2023 by Israeli engineers Moshe Shalev and brothers Dean and Orian Leitersdorf, makes software that makes AI models run more efficiently on chips and develops real-time world models such as Lucy and Oasis; it was an early user of Amazon's Trainium3 chips and was valued at about $4 billion in May after a $300 million round that included Sequoia and Nvidia. According to the FT, owning Decart would help Amazon Web Services compete with Nvidia by freeing up chip capacity. Anthropic reportedly explored buying Decart for about $6 billion in August before walking away.

Why it matters

It would be one of Amazon's largest AI acquisitions and a direct bet that software efficiency on its in-house Trainium chips can loosen Nvidia's grip on AI compute.

Amazon קרובה לעסקה של 7.5 מיליארד דולר לרכישת סטארט-אפ ייעול ה-AI Decart, לפי FT

2026-11-21 רכישות amazon aws שבבים

Amazon נמצאת במגעים מתקדמים לרכישת Decart תמורת כ-7.5 מיליארד דולר, ועסקה עשויה להיות מוכרזת כבר השבוע, כך דיווח ה-Financial Times ביום שבת, 21 בנובמבר; Amazon לא הגיבה ו-Decart סירבה להגיב. ה-Wall Street Journal דיווח לראשונה על המגעים באוקטובר, לפי שווי של כ-7 מיליארד דולר.
