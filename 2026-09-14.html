The AI-slowdown debate escalates: Microsoft joins the pacing camp, two more safety researchers defect to METR, and Trump publicly rejects the calls

A voluntary pacing commitment among competing labs only means something if it survives contact with competitive and political pressure — a major cross-industry partner joining within a day, safety staff leaving to become outside watchdogs, and the U.S. president publicly waving off the idea all happened in the same 24-hour window, testing how durable the "slow down" moment actually is.

Anthropic tells investors it expects a second straight quarter of operating profit ahead of its IPO

2026-09-13 business IPO finance update

What's new since Anthropic's Q2 results (reported in August): the company privately told a small group of shareholders, per the Financial Times, that it expects to report an adjusted operating profit again this quarter — a second consecutive profitable quarter — with gross margins above 80% before accounting for revenue-sharing payments to partners like Amazon and model-training costs. The disclosure comes as Anthropic prepares for an IPO reportedly aiming to match or exceed SpaceX's record $86.3 billion offering, building on a Q2 that saw revenue top $11.5 billion, more than fourteen times its year-earlier pace.

Why it matters
