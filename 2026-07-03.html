OpenAI previews GPT-5.6 family, but only to a government-vetted partner list

2026-07-01/02 models frontier labs policy

OpenAI began a limited preview of its next model generation — GPT-5.6 Sol (flagship), Terra (balanced mid-tier), and Luna (fast, low-cost) — pricing them at $5/$30, $2.50/$15, and $1/$6 per million input/output tokens respectively. Unlike prior launches, access is restricted to a small group of "trusted partners" whose participation has been disclosed to the U.S. government, with general availability planned only "in the coming weeks." OpenAI says GPT-5.6 Sol advances the frontier on software engineering, computer use, scientific research, and cybersecurity, and is being positioned as the company's most capable model yet for vulnerability research and exploitation tasks.

Why it matters

The gated rollout — tied to government coordination rather than a standard staged release — signals that frontier-model deployment is increasingly being shaped by national-security review processes, not just commercial readiness.

Abu Dhabi's MGX closes $49B fund, one of the largest AI-dedicated funds ever raised

2026-07-01 funding infrastructure sovereign capital

MGX, the Abu Dhabi investment firm anchored by Mubadala and G42, closed its first dedicated AI fund at $49 billion, exceeding its original $45 billion target. The fund spans the AI stack — semiconductors, data-center infrastructure, and AI platforms — and has already backed 14 companies. MGX previously co-led Anthropic's $30 billion raise and OpenAI's $122 billion round, and participated in xAI's $20 billion raise, making this fund close a continuation of its role as one of the most active capital sources behind frontier AI labs.

Why it matters

The scale of the raise underscores how much of frontier AI's growth is now being financed by sovereign capital from the Gulf, concentrating influence over the industry's infrastructure buildout among a small number of state-linked investors.

Together AI raises $800M Series C at $8.3B valuation, more than doubling its worth

2026-07-01 funding open-weight infrastructure

Together AI raised $800 million in a round led by Aramco Ventures, pushing its valuation to $8.3 billion — more than double its $3.3 billion valuation from early 2025. The company, which runs training and inference infrastructure for open models like DeepSeek, MiniMax, and Kimi, reported annual bookings crossed $1.15 billion last quarter as open-model usage across the industry roughly tripled over twelve months. Investors also included Vista Equity Partners, General Catalyst, and Nvidia.

Why it matters

The raise is a concrete data point in the broader shift of enterprise AI spend toward open-weight models served on independent infrastructure, rather than exclusively through closed-model APIs.
