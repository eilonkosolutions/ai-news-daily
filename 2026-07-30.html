Meta's stock sinks 9%+ as AI capex guidance rises to $145B and free cash flow craters

2026-07-29 earnings ai-capex big-tech markets

Meta reported Q2 2026 revenue of $60.8 billion, up 28% year-over-year and ahead of estimates, but EPS fell to $6.18 from $7.14 a year earlier as total expenses jumped 55% to $42.03 billion (including $2.4 billion in legal charges and $1.18 billion in severance). Free cash flow collapsed 91% to $784 million from $8.55 billion a year ago. Meta also raised its 2026 AI capital-expenditure guidance to a range of $130–145 billion, up from $125–145 billion previously. Shares fell roughly 9–10% in after-hours trading.

Why it matters

Meta's results crystallize investor anxiety that hyperscaler AI spending is outrunning near-term returns, adding pressure on every major lab and cloud provider to show a payoff for record capital outlays.

FT: Google DeepMind has dismantled its Nobel-winning AlphaFold team

2026-07-29 research deepmind talent biotech

The Financial Times reported that Google DeepMind has dismantled the dedicated team behind AlphaFold, its Nobel Prize-winning protein-structure model, with most of the paper's original authors reassigned over the past year to Gemini work, enzyme design, nuclear fusion, and genomics, or moved to Alphabet's Isomorphic Labs spinoff. Close to a quarter of the original AlphaFold authors have left Google entirely, including 2024 Nobel laureate John Jumper, who departed for Anthropic in June along with fellow AlphaFold contributors Jonas Adler and Alexander Pritzel.

Why it matters

The unwinding of DeepMind's flagship scientific-AI team signals a broader industry pull of research talent and resources toward large language models and agentic products, even as rivals like Anthropic court scientists for open-ended research work.

OpenAI launches free frontier-model access for 100,000 academic researchers

2026-07-29 research access openai science

OpenAI launched "ChatGPT for Academic Researchers," giving academic scientists free access to its most advanced models, including GPT-5.6 Sol Pro, starting with 10,000 researchers this summer and scaling to 100,000 by 2027. Participants get business-grade-privacy workspaces (no training on their data by default), can invite up to four institutional collaborators, and receive hands-on onboarding support; early access is already live at institutions including the Institute for Advanced Study and École normale supérieure. The program is part of a commitment of more than $250 million to external scientific research through 2027.

Why it matters

It's the clearest sign yet that a frontier lab sees wide, low-cost researcher access to top models as a lever for accelerating scientific discovery — and as a goodwill play amid mounting scrutiny of AI's costs and safety incidents.
