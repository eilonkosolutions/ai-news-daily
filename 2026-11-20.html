Anthropic opens 34% above IPO price in Nasdaq debut, closes up 17% as broader market slides

US requires licenses for AI chips to five countries and closes China's remote-access loophole

2026-11-19 policy export-controls chips catch-up

The Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security issued an interim final rule on Nov. 16, listed for Federal Register publication and effect on Nov. 19, that requires licenses for exports of advanced AI chips to Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil, which it says carry a higher-than-normal risk of diversion. The rule also closes a loophole that let Chinese companies rent computing power on US chips from data centers in third countries, requiring licenses for facilities that would provide large amounts of compute to China-parented firms. Shipments to US-headquartered customers in the five countries are exempt if exporters file monthly reports, and the rule formally rescinds the Biden-era AI Diffusion Rule. Coverage tied the timing to scrutiny of chip flows to China following Jensen Huang's resignation as Nvidia CEO; sources differ on whether parts of the rule took effect immediately or on publication.

Why it matters

Southeast Asia has become a major hub for AI data-center construction, and the rule puts US licensing between those buildouts and the chips that power them, while targeting the rented-compute route China has used to sidestep earlier controls.

Isomorphic Labs raises $5 billion Series C to push AI-designed drugs toward human trials

2026-11-17 funding drug-discovery alphabet catch-up

Isomorphic Labs, the Alphabet-backed AI drug-discovery company spun out of Google DeepMind, announced a $5 billion Series C on Nov. 17, co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital with participation from Andreessen Horowitz and other new investors. Alphabet made a significant investment and all existing investors returned. The round comes about six months after a $2.1 billion Series B in May and brings total external funding to roughly $7.7 billion. The company did not disclose a valuation; Bloomberg had reported on Nov. 4 that talks valued it at about $50 billion. Isomorphic says the money will fund advanced biology models, partnerships and the work needed to take its drug candidates into clinical trials.

Why it matters

It is one of the largest private rounds ever for an AI-for-science company, and a bet that frontier-scale AI can shorten the path from molecule design to the clinic.

2026-11-17 גיוס הון גילוי תרופות alphabet השלמה

Isomorphic Labs, חברת גילוי התרופות מבוססת ה-AI בגיבוי Alphabet שיצאה מ-Google DeepMind, הודיעה ב-17 בנובמבר על סבב C בהיקף 5 מיליארד דולר, בהובלה משותפת של Greenoaks ו-Sequoia Capital ובהשתתפות Andreessen Horowitz ומשקיעים חדשים נוספים.
