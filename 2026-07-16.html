Anthropic lines up IPO investor meetings, could list as soon as October

2026-07-15 funding ipo anthropic

Bankers from Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, and JPMorgan Chase have begun scheduling preliminary investor meetings for Anthropic ahead of a possible IPO, positioning the company for a listing as soon as October 2026, according to a CNBC report citing people familiar with the matter. The meetings are a standard early step where underwriters gauge investor demand before a formal roadshow and share sale. Anthropic confidentially filed its IPO prospectus with the SEC last month and closed a $65 billion funding round in May at a $965 billion valuation, edging past OpenAI's roughly $852 billion valuation in the same period. No exchange, ticker, or firm listing date has been confirmed.

Why it matters

A successful Anthropic IPO would rank among the largest tech listings in years and would be the first real public-market test of AI-lab valuations that have so far been set almost entirely in illiquid private and secondary markets.

xAI open-sources Grok Build after repo-upload privacy backlash

2026-07-15 security open-source xai

Update since yesterday's report: xAI released the full source code of its Grok Build coding CLI on GitHub under an Apache 2.0 license on July 15, days after independent researchers found the tool was silently uploading entire user code repositories — including unredacted secrets — to xAI-controlled cloud storage. The open-source release lets developers inspect exactly how the harness assembles context and dispatches tool calls, and lets it be run fully local-first against a user's own inference setup. xAI also reset usage limits for all users the same day, saying a caching bug had been causing people to hit their limits faster than intended.

Why it matters

Open-sourcing the client is a concrete, independently verifiable response to the trust problem the upload scandal created, raising the bar for how AI coding tools are expected to prove they aren't secretly exfiltrating user code.

China's AI-companion rules take effect, forcing Doubao and Qwen to shut down personalized chat features

2026-07-15 policy regulation china

China's Interim Measures for the Administration of Anthropomorphic AI Interaction Services — issued by the Cyberspace Administration of China and four partner agencies in April — took effect July 15, immediately forcing ByteDance's Doubao and Alibaba's Qwen to shut down personalized AI-companion and custom-persona features used by hundreds of millions of people. The rules require providers of human-like or emotionally interactive AI to register with regulators, pass a security assessment, clearly disclose that users are talking to an AI, add anti-addiction and crisis-intervention safeguards, and obtain parental consent for users under 14. They apply to any company serving users inside China regardless of where it is based; Tencent, Alibaba, and NetEase had already pulled comparable features in the weeks leading up to the deadline.

Why it matters

It's one of the first binding national regulations anywhere targeting AI companion and anthropomorphic-interaction products specifically, and its immediate disruption of two of China's most-used AI apps shows regulators are willing to force real product changes rather than issue guidance alone.

xAI פותחת את קוד המקור של Grok Build בעקבות סערת ההעלאה השקטה של מאגרי קוד

2026-07-15 אבטחה קוד פתוח xai

עדכון מאז הדיווח של אתמול: ב-15 ביולי פרסמה xAI את קוד המקור המלא של כלי הקידוד שלה Grok Build ב-GitHub תחת רישיון Apache 2.0, ימים לאחר שחוקרים עצמאיים גילו שהכלי העלה בשקט מאגרי קוד שלמים של משתמשים — כולל סודות (secrets) לא מוסתרים — לאחסון ענן בשליטת xAI. הפרסום בקוד פתוח מאפשר למפתחים לבחון בדיוק כיצד המנגנון בונה הקשר (context) ומפעיל קריאות כלים, ולהריץ אותו באופן מקומי לחלוטין מול תשתית ההיסקה (inference) של המשתמש עצמו. xAI גם איפסה באותו יום את מגבלות השימוש עבור כלל המשתמשים, בטענה שבאג במנגנון המטמון (caching) גרם למשתמשים להגיע למגבלה מהר מהצפוי.

למה זה חשוב

פתיחת קוד המקור של הכלי היא תגובה קונקרטית וניתנת לאימות עצמאי לבעיית האמון שיצרה פרשת ההעלאות, והיא מעלה את הרף לגבי האופן שבו כלי קידוד מבוססי AI נדרשים להוכיח שהם אינם מדליפים בסתר את קוד המשתמשים.
