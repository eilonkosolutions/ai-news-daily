Alibaba launches Qwen3.8-Max, its largest model yet, claiming benchmark parity with Anthropic and OpenAI

2026-08-03 models china open-weight enterprise

Alibaba released Qwen3.8-Max on Monday, its most capable model to date: a 2.4 trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model activating roughly 95 billion parameters per query, with a 1-million-token context window and multimodal handling of text, code, images, video, and documents. Alibaba published benchmarks — including 86.6 on Terminal-Bench 2.1, 67.7 on SWE-bench Pro, and 92.6 on GPQA Diamond — that it says put the model ahead of Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 and OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol on several coding, agentic, and multimodal evaluations. The model is live now via QwenCloud and Alibaba's new QwenWork enterprise-agent platform, with open weights due next week — Alibaba's first open-source release at this scale. Alibaba shares rose about 4.5% in New York premarket trading and 7% in Hong Kong on the news.

Why it matters

Coming three weeks after Moonshot's Kimi K3, it's the second Chinese lab in a month to publish benchmarks claiming rough parity with the leading closed US models — reinforcing that China's AI push is now increasingly the largest, not just the cheapest, open-weight option.

Enterprise AI vendor Yellow.ai to go public via $550M SPAC merger with Bluerock Acquisition Corp.

2026-08-03 funding enterprise-ai ipo agents

Yellow.ai, an enterprise agentic-AI platform, agreed to go public through a merger with blank-check company Bluerock Acquisition Corp. (Nasdaq: BLRK), with the combined company set to trade as "YAI." The deal implies a roughly $300 million pre-money valuation for Yellow.ai and a pro forma equity value near $550 million, and is expected to bring in over $200 million in gross proceeds — about $175 million from Bluerock's trust account plus roughly $30 million of committed private investment, assuming no shareholder redemptions. Yellow.ai says it handles 16 billion conversations a year for more than 650 enterprise clients across 85 countries, with over $34 million in unaudited revenue last fiscal year. The deal is expected to close in the second half of 2026.

Why it matters

It's one of the first SPAC routes to the public markets for an enterprise agentic-AI vendor, testing investor appetite for AI-native software outside the mega-cap labs and offering a possible template for other AI startups seeking a listing without a traditional IPO.
