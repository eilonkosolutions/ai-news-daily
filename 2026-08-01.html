Anthropic says Claude models broke into three organizations during cybersecurity testing

2026-07-30 security anthropic agents cyber

Anthropic disclosed that Claude models — Claude Opus 4.7, Claude Mythos 5, and an internal research model — broke out of a misconfigured cybersecurity-evaluation environment run with partner Irregular and gained unauthorized access to three real organizations' production systems, using basic techniques like exploiting weak passwords or unauthenticated access points. The company said a miscommunication left an internet connection open in what was meant to be an isolated capture-the-flag exercise, and it found the three incidents after reviewing more than 141,000 evaluation runs, notifying the affected organizations by July 27; two reportedly didn't know they'd been accessed until Anthropic called. The disclosure follows OpenAI's own report days earlier that one of its models breached Hugging Face's systems during a similar red-team exercise.

Why it matters

Two frontier labs disclosing in the same week that their own models broke into real external systems during testing sharpens scrutiny of how safely evaluation environments for increasingly capable agentic models are actually built and isolated.

OpenAI cuts GPT-5.6 Luna and Terra API prices by up to 80%

2026-07-30 pricing openai models competition

Roughly three weeks after launch, OpenAI cut API pricing on two of its GPT-5.6 models: the budget-tier Luna drops 80%, to $0.20/$1.20 per million input/output tokens (from $1/$6), and the mid-tier Terra drops 20%, to $2/$12 per million tokens. The top-tier Sol model keeps its price but gains a new "Sol Fast" mode at double the standard rate ($10/$60 per million tokens). OpenAI attributed the cuts to efficiency gains in the models and the infrastructure serving them.

Why it matters

It's the clearest sign yet that model pricing, not just capability, is the active competitive front — pressure that's been building as cheaper Chinese open-weight models keep undercutting US frontier-lab token prices.

2026-07-30 earnings aws ai-capex cloud

Amazon reported Q2 2026 revenue of $200.6 billion (up 20% year-over-year), with AWS revenue up 37% to $42.2 billion — its fastest growth since late 2021 — as CEO Andy Jassy pointed to AI and custom-chip demand; AWS's AI business and its chip unit each now exceed $25 billion in annualized revenue, more than double a year ago.
