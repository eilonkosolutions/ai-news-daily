Xi Jinping delivers first-ever WAIC keynote, pitches China-led AI governance body for the Global South

2026-07-17 policy china ai-governance

Update since our July 14 report that Xi Jinping was scheduled to speak: he delivered the keynote address at the opening of the 2026 World AI Conference in Shanghai on July 17, his first appearance at the event since it launched in 2018. Xi called for a "people-centred" approach to AI development and warned against "new historical injustices" arising from unequal access to the technology, pledging cooperation with African, Latin American, Asian, and BRICS nations on AI capacity-building. He said AI development "should not be a solo performance by a single country, but a symphony of international cooperation." Analysts read the speech as giving concrete shape to China's proposed World AI Cooperation Organization, a body it wants headquartered in Shanghai to offer developing nations a seat that Western-led AI governance frameworks have not. The conference runs through July 20 alongside a High-Level Meeting on Global AI Governance.

Why it matters

It's the clearest signal yet that China is positioning itself as an alternative pole for global AI governance aimed at the Global South, opening a second front in the US-China AI rivalry beyond model and chip competition.

Anthropic, Blackstone, and Hellman & Friedman launch Ode, a $1.5B enterprise AI implementation firm

2026-07-15 funding enterprise anthropic

Anthropic, private-equity firms Blackstone and Hellman & Friedman, and a consortium including Goldman Sachs, General Atlantic, Apollo Global Management, GIC, and Sequoia Capital launched Ode with Anthropic on July 15, a standalone $1.5 billion enterprise AI services company built on Fractional AI, the applied-AI consulting firm Anthropic acquired in May. Led by Fractional co-founders Chris Taylor (CEO) and Eddie Siegel (CTO), Ode pairs roughly 100 engineers with Anthropic's frontier models under a "Claude-first" mandate to help midsize enterprises move past pilots and embed AI into daily operations. Taylor told TechCrunch he sees a path to Ode becoming a trillion-dollar company if it executes well.

Why it matters

It's a bet that the next big pool of AI money is in selling the work of implementing AI inside enterprises rather than just model access, and it gives Anthropic a captive, well-capitalized services arm to compete with the consulting practices built around OpenAI and Microsoft.

DeepMind and Isomorphic Labs publish joint framework to prevent and counter AI-enabled bio-threats

2026-07-16 research biosecurity safety

Google DeepMind and Isomorphic Labs published a joint bioresilience framework on July 16 built around three pillars: adapting DeepMind's SynthID watermarking technology to flag AI-generated biological sequences so DNA-synthesis providers can screen risky orders; using the AlphaEvolve optimization system to speed up metagenomic sequencing analysis for faster pathogen detection; and a dedicated Isomorphic Labs unit that can rapidly deploy its drug-design engine to develop countermeasures during an outbreak. The companies say they have built more than 15 partnerships over the past year with groups including Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, the UK AI Security Institute, CEPI, and the Francis Crick Institute.

Why it matters

It's a concrete attempt by a frontier lab to address one of the most-cited catastrophic risks of powerful AI — biological-weapon design uplift — using the same underlying model technology, rather than only flagging the risk in a safety report.

2026-07-16 מחקר ביו-אבטחה בטיחות
